We cover the full cycle of working with artificial intelligence — from the initial audit to supporting a running solution.

### 1. AI implementation audit and strategy

- Business process analysis and finding growth opportunities with AI
- Data assessment: what you have, what is missing, how to collect it
- Implementation roadmap prioritized by impact and cost

### 2. AI solution development

- **LLM chatbots and assistants** — customer support, internal knowledge bases, employee assistants
- **Document analysis** — data extraction, classification, summarization, semantic search
- **Computer vision** — quality control, object recognition, video analytics
- **Predictive analytics** — demand, churn, risks, recommendations

### 3. Implementation and integration

- Integration with CRM, ERP, accounting systems and telephony
- Cloud or on-premise deployment, working inside the client's environment
- Load testing and fault tolerance

### 4. Support

- Model quality monitoring and retraining
- Client team training, documentation
- Solution development for new business tasks
