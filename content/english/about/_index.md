Project AI is a team of engineers, analysts and developers that helps businesses use artificial intelligence in practice, not in presentations.

We started with in-house AI projects and grew into a full-cycle company: from business process audits and prototyping to production deployment and support. Our specialty is applied AI: large language models, document analysis, computer vision, predictive analytics and decision automation.

### Our principles

- **Measurable results** — every project starts with metrics and ends with a proven impact.
- **AI for the task, not the other way around** — we don't push models where simple automation is enough.
- **Security and transparency** — we work inside the client's environment, with open code and documentation.
- **Long-term partnership** — we support the solution after launch and develop it together with the business.

We work with companies in finance, retail, logistics, manufacturing and IT. If you want to understand where AI will bring the biggest impact to your business — write to us, we'll discuss it with no obligations.
